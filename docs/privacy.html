Daily Reset (每日重置) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Daily Reset is a daily checklist app made by Moon (moonsiao). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics and no network access.

Data stored on your device

Your tasks, check marks and settings are saved only on your own phone, inside the app's private storage. They never leave your device unless you copy them yourself using the "Backup / Restore" text feature. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

The app only uses vibration (a short buzz when you tick a task). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/moonsiao/daily-checklist.

隐私政策（中文）

每日重置不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人资料。没有账号、没有广告、没有分析、不连网。所有任务和设定只存在你自己的手机里，卸载 App 就会删除。App 只使用震动权限。

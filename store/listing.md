## Full description
EN:
Daily Reset is a simple checklist for the things you do every day — workouts, water, vitamins, study, game dailies, anything.

• Daily tasks come back every day, ticks clear automatically
• Today-only tasks that disappear tomorrow
• Tasks that need several times a day: tap to count (e.g. water 5/8)
• Choose your own reset time — 2am for night owls, or match your game's daily reset
• Home screen widget shows today's progress at a glance
• Works offline, no account, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your phone
• Backup / restore your task list with one copy-paste
